Act as a Senior Multilingual NLP Specialist, Yoruba Language Annotator and AI Translation Quality Evaluator.

Build a complete GitHub project titled:

**Nigerian Language AI Translation Quality – English, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin**

### PROJECT SCENARIO

An AI technology company is developing a multilingual translation system for Nigerian users.

The system translates English content into Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin.

However, human reviewers have discovered several problems:

- Yoruba translations sound unnatural.
- Nigerian Pidgin translations are sometimes grammatically incorrect.
- English idioms are translated literally.
- Cultural expressions lose their original meaning.
- Informal conversations are translated as formal statements.
- Some translations introduce meanings that were not present in the original text.

You have been assigned to evaluate the translation system and produce corrected training examples.

### YOUR ASSIGNMENT

**1. Translation Error Identification**

Create examples involving:
- Incorrect word selection.
- Literal translation.
- Grammatical errors.
- Contextual misunderstanding.
- Cultural expressions.
- Idiomatic expressions.
- Ambiguous sentences.
- Tone inconsistencies.

**2. Multilingual Correction**

For every example, provide:

- Original English sentence.
- Original AI-generated Yoruba translation.
- Original AI-generated Pidgin translation.
- Identified errors.
- Corrected Yoruba translation.
- Corrected Nigerian Pidgin translation.
- Corrected English meaning.
- Explanation of the corrections.

**3. Realistic Language Scenarios**

Include:
- Nigerian greetings.
- Family conversations.
- Educational communication.
- Market transactions.
- Workplace conversations.
- Customer interactions.
- Social media expressions.
- Everyday Nigerian experiences.

**4. Translation Quality Scoring**

Evaluate translations using a 1–5 scale for:

- Meaning preservation.
- Grammatical correctness.
- Naturalness.
- Cultural relevance.
- Contextual accuracy.
- Fluency.

Include examples where a translation is grammatically correct but communicates the wrong meaning.

**5. Human Review Process**

Create a review workflow involving:

- Initial annotation.
- Translation correction.
- Independent review.
- Reviewer disagreement.
- Final approved translation.

Document how disagreements are resolved.

Create:
- At least 60 multilingual translation examples.

Use standard Yoruba orthography and natural Nigerian Pidgin.

The finished repository must demonstrate practical multilingual annotation and translation quality evaluation skills.
