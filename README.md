**NIGERIAN MULTILINGUAL AI CUSTOMER SUPPORT TRAINING DATA ANNOTATION AND EVALUATION**

### PROJECT SCENARIO

A fintech company is developing an AI-powered customer support assistant for Nigerian customers. The assistant communicates in English, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin.

During internal testing, the company discovers that its AI assistant frequently misunderstands Nigerian expressions, gives incorrect financial support information, produces unnatural translations and responds inappropriately to frustrated customers.

You have been assigned as a Remote Language AI Trainer to review and improve the quality of the AI-generated responses.

### YOUR ASSIGNMENT

Create a realistic annotation project covering the following problems:

**1. Contextual Language Errors**
- Misinterpretation of Nigerian Pidgin.
- Incorrect interpretation of Yoruba expressions.
- Literal translations that change intended meaning.
- Incorrect understanding of Nigerian conversational expressions.

**2. AI Response Correction**
- Identify incorrect AI-generated responses.
- Explain the errors.
- Produce corrected English responses.
- Provide natural Yoruba translations.
- Provide natural Nigerian Pidgin translations.

**3. Customer Support Evaluation**
Create customer support scenarios involving:
- Failed bank transfers.
- Delayed transactions.
- Account access problems.
- Payment confirmation.
- Refund requests.
- Unsuccessful airtime purchases.
- Customer complaints.

**4. Response Preference Ranking**

Generate two alternative AI responses for each scenario.

Score both responses from 1–5 based on:
- Accuracy.
- Helpfulness.
- Contextual understanding.
- Tone.
- Clarity.
- Instruction following.

Select the better response and explain why.

**5. Quality Assurance**

Introduce a second-reviewer process to identify:
- Incorrect corrections.
- Unnatural Yoruba.
- Incorrect Pidgin expressions.
- Meaning inconsistencies.
- Unsupported financial claims.

### REQUIRED DELIVERABLES

Create:
- At least 50 synthetic customer conversations.
- At least 30 response evaluation records.
- English, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin annotations.
- Original AI responses and corrected responses.
- Annotation guidelines.
- Evaluation scoring rubric.
- Quality review records.
- Python validation scripts.
- Jupyter Notebook analysis.
- Visual quality reports.

### IMPORTANT

Do not use real financial information or actual customer data.

All examples must be synthetic.

The finished repository must demonstrate how a language annotator improves multilingual AI customer support responses.
